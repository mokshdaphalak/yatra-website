Discover the Magic of Jaipur

Royal palaces, colourful markets and unforgettable experiences.

Welcome to the Pink City

Jaipur is a beautiful city in Rajasthan, famous for its royal history, magnificent forts, traditional handicrafts and delicious local food.


Whether you love history, photography, shopping or exploring local culture, Jaipur has something special for every traveller.


Plan Your Journey

Must-Visit Places

Add these famous attractions to your travel list.

Hawa Mahal

Admire the iconic palace and its beautiful windows.

Amber Fort

Explore grand courtyards and historic architecture.

Local Markets

Discover colourful textiles, jewellery and crafts.

Travel Tips

Best time: October to March is generally pleasant for sightseeing.

Local transport: Taxis, buses, auto-rickshaws and metro services.

Food to try: Dal baati churma, ghewar and pyaaz kachori.

Travel tip: Carry water and comfortable footwear for sightseeing.
